## OWASP Juice Shop

| | |
|---|---|
| Upstream | https://github.com/juice-shop/juice-shop |
| Copyright | © 2014–2026 Bjoern Kimminich & the OWASP Juice Shop contributors |
| License | MIT — full text at `target-apps/juice-shop/LICENSE` |
| Vendored at | `target-apps/juice-shop/` and `target-apps/juice-shop-blind/` |

Two copies are vendored, and neither is a pristine upstream checkout:

- **`target-apps/juice-shop/`** — the working copy. Produced from a pristine
  clone by `tools/blind-development/split_answer_key.py`, which removes the
  markers that give the vulnerabilities away: `vuln-code-snippet` tags, the
  `codefixes/` minigame data, `SOLUTIONS.md`, the refactoring-safety-net
  tooling, two tests that embed exact vulnerable line numbers, and the criteria
  argument of every `solveIf()` oracle call. The vulnerabilities themselves are
  untouched.
- **`target-apps/juice-shop-blind/`** — the above, plus the `category` field of
  all 113 challenge entries replaced with a neutral placeholder. This is the
  copy the scanner reads.

Both are **modified** copies of MIT-licensed work, redistributed under that same
MIT license with the upstream copyright notice preserved in place. Neither is
suitable as a source for Juice Shop itself — get that from upstream.

Juice Shop is deliberately vulnerable software. It is vendored here as a
measurement target and must not be deployed anywhere reachable.

## Archived third-party scanner output

`results/archive/2026-07-five-tool-benchmark/raw/` contains output produced by
five external open-source security scanners during a July 2026 comparison. The
files are those tools' own output, generated by running them against the
vendored Juice Shop copy; each tool remains subject to its own license and
terms. They are retained as the provenance for design decisions still in force
and are not presented as this project's work.
